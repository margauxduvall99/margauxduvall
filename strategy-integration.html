Case Study 03

Marketing Strategy Integration

A broad marketing role spanning brand visibility, campaign execution, and internal reporting across a law firm and its partner companies.

Client Gertsburg Licata Firm ↗
Partner brands Gertsburg Licata Talent, Gertsburg Licata M&A, CoverMySix ↗
Tools WordPress, Zoominfo, Mailchimp, Canva, Adobe, Excel, Google Analytics, Typeform

During my time at Gertsburg Licata Firm, I engaged in a diverse range of marketing activities working closely with partner companies — brand visibility campaigns, collaborative outreach, digital platform management, visual asset creation, website management, content creation, new-hire onboarding support, and internal reporting to inform strategy.

Representing the Firm and Its Partners

I acted as a liaison between GL Firm and its partner companies, including Gertsburg Licata Talent (executive search), Gertsburg Licata Mergers & Acquisitions, and CoverMySix Legal Risk Assessment.

Campaign Development & Outreach

I led the conceptualizing, developing, and executing of marketing campaigns to increase brand visibility for the firm and its partners, and worked closely with other teams on outreach — leveraging platforms like Zoominfo and Mailchimp for effective communication and engagement.

Social & Content

I managed all social channels and created visually appealing assets — graphics, video, and more — using Canva and Adobe to enhance the firm's online presence, along with promotional materials like webinar resources, presentation decks, and flyers built to brand guidelines.

Website & Onboarding

Using WordPress, I kept the firm's websites current and user-friendly, and contributed blog posts and educational articles. I also supported new-employee onboarding — scheduling headshots, writing bios, and handling GL merchandise for a smoother integration into the team.

Reporting & Analytics

I managed internal marketing reporting using Excel, Google Analytics, and Typeform, surfacing the insights that informed the team's strategic decisions and drove continuous improvement.
