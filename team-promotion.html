Case Study 04

Team Promotion — BW Women's Soccer

As sole Social Media Specialist, I built the team's Instagram from scratch — a recruiting tool, a culture showcase, and a season-long content calendar all in one.

Client Baldwin Wallace University Women's Soccer
Role Social Media Specialist
Tools Procreate, Adobe Creative Cloud Express, Adobe Premiere Pro, Canva
Link @bw_wsoccer ↗

As the Social Media Specialist for the Baldwin Wallace University Women's Soccer team, I took over full responsibility of the Instagram account and created supplemental content for Twitter. As a player on the team myself, my coaches came to me for guidance on trends and opportunities, knowing I'd already done social media for small businesses and other clients. This project was meant to build an initial, cohesive presence, highlight team strengths and success, and double as a recruiting tool.

My Role & Responsibilities

Working closely with Head Coach Jim Wojtkun, I curated the team's digital presence around the specific elements of team culture we wanted to showcase — a mix of photos, video, and story content. I planned and executed a content calendar that structured our posting schedule and thematic direction, while staying ahead of emerging trends in the social landscape.

I also revitalized the page from the ground up, revamping outdated content to match a newly defined vision. In total, I produced and curated roughly 150 posts, videos, and stories — each built to resonate with our audience and celebrate the team's achievements.

My Tools

I worked primarily in Procreate, Adobe Creative Cloud Express, Adobe Premiere Pro, and Canva. My teammates were a huge asset too, participating in interviews, photoshoots, and Instagram takeovers — this was a project I largely ran solo, which meant leveling up several skills along the way.

My Results

0+ Followers (from low 300s)
0 Avg. likes (from ~50)
0+ Posts, videos & stories produced

Followers went from the low 300s to over 700 in a year, and average likes climbed from around 50 to around 150. Coach Wojtkun and the team gave strong feedback on seeing their successes highlighted, and several recruits told me the team's active social presence made them more interested in the program and gave them a better sense of the BW community.

Photos and video shot and edited by Margaux, with thanks to the BW Women's Soccer staff, sports photographers, and teammates.
