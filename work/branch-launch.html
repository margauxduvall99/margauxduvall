Case Study 05

Branch Launch — West Bank Golf Club

Helping launch downtown Cleveland's first indoor golf simulator and bar — social, website, and go-to-market strategy for opening day.

Client West Bank Golf Club ↗
Channels Instagram ↗ · Facebook ↗
Press Cleveland.com ↗
BW News ↗

I assisted the West Bank Golf Club in their grand opening by creating social media posts for Instagram and Facebook, as well as helping build and manage their website. I also helped strategize marketing tactics ahead of their spring opening in Cleveland's Flats district.

The launch was covered by Cleveland.com and BW News, both featuring the Baldwin Wallace alumni team spearheading the venture — with a bit more on my role in getting the word out.
