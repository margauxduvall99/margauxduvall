Case Study 01

Channel Community Digital Presence

Website, email, and event experiences, built to keep Channel Focus connected all year round.

Client Channel Focus
Role Senior Marketing Manager
Timeframe 2023 — Present
Tools WordPress, Cvent, Canva, Adobe Creative Cloud
Links Channel Focus Community ↗
Baptie & Co. ↗

This project showcases my work with Channel Focus, where I help lead digital marketing initiatives to support event promotion, audience engagement, and brand consistency. From managing the company website and email campaigns to designing event experiences through Cvent, I played a key role in elevating the organization's digital presence across channels.

My Role at a Glance

  • Design — visuals both digitally and for event displays
  • App design collaboration on the event platform
  • Email marketing strategy & design
  • Website updates & design
  • Social campaigns & content curation

For each event, I use Cvent to design an interactive event app that helps attendees navigate the venue, learn about sessions and speakers, build a personalized schedule, and connect with other attendees.

Social Media

I develop content daily — both visuals and copy — for event promos, speaker highlights, and engagement posts, tracking impressions, engagement rate, and click-throughs along the way.
