Case Study 02

Event Promotion — Accelerate Pitch Competition

Leading a student team to promote the Cleveland Leadership Center's flagship pitch competition, where participants compete for seed money to launch initiatives that improve Cleveland.

Client Cleveland Leadership Center ↗
Role Account Executive, Digital Marketing Center of Baldwin Wallace
Tools Basecamp, Google Docs & Sheets, WordPress, Canva

The Digital Marketing Center of Baldwin Wallace partnered with the Cleveland Leadership Center — a leadership development organization in Northeast Ohio — to promote Accelerate, their annual pitch competition. Our goal was to drive pitch submissions and get people thinking outside the box about how to improve their city.

As Account Executive, I organized and led a team of students developing social posts across platforms, writing blog content, and consulting with the client on website improvements that would make the submission process more fluid.

My Responsibilities

I oversaw all team operations, reviewed and approved posts and blogs, and was the primary point of communication with the client. On a weekly basis, that meant reviewing, giving feedback on, and editing posts for Facebook, LinkedIn, Twitter, and Instagram, then scheduling everything against an established content calendar. I also wrote one of the campaign's blog posts myself.

My Tools

The team ran on Basecamp for communication and organization — meeting briefs, follow-ups, and to-dos all lived there, which mattered given the project was fully virtual with a lot of moving deliverables. Blog posts were drafted and reviewed in Google Docs, then formatted for the client in WordPress. Social posts across LinkedIn, Instagram, Twitter, and Facebook were designed in Canva and tracked in a content calendar in Google Sheets, where captions, links, and tags went through review before scheduling.

My Results

The team produced six blog posts and more than 50 social posts across channels, all linking back to the client's website or campaign articles to drive traffic and organic reach.
